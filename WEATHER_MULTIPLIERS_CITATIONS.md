## How Multipliers Were Derived

### Rain (1.35)
- **Source:** Scientific Reports 2025
- **Basis:** 32-38% average increase (using 35% = 1.35 multiplier)
- **Supporting:** Texas study showing up to 57% increase

### Heavy Rain (1.45)
- **Source:** Scientific Reports 2025
- **Basis:** 36-52% increase for heavy rain (>25 mm/hr), using 45% = 1.45 multiplier

### Snow (1.6)
- **Source:** ETRR 2022 + Accident Analysis & Prevention 2017
- **Basis:** Largest relative risk increase; 24x fatal crashes for multi-vehicle incidents
- **Calculation:** Conservative estimate accounting for general snow conditions

### Heavy Snow (2.0)
- **Source:** Accident Analysis & Prevention 2017
- **Basis:** 24x more likely for fatal multi-vehicle crashes
- **Calculation:** 2.0x multiplier reflects severe snow conditions

### Fog (2.5)
- **Source:** Accident Analysis & Prevention 2017
- **Basis:** 35x more likely for fatal multi-vehicle crashes
- **Calculation:** 2.5x multiplier reflects extreme fog danger (conservative vs 35x for fatal crashes)

### Sleet (1.9)
- **Source:** Derived from snow + ice research
- **Basis:** Combines snow risk (1.6) with additional ice risk
- **Calculation:** Intermediate between snow (1.6) and heavy snow (2.0)

### Thunderstorm (1.6)
- **Source:** Derived from rain + visibility + wind research
- **Basis:** Combines rain (1.35) with visibility and wind effects
- **Calculation:** Slightly higher than rain due to multiple hazards

### Mist (1.15)
- **Source:** Derived from visibility research
- **Basis:** Moderate visibility reduction
- **Calculation:** Lower than fog due to less severe conditions

### Cloudy (1.02)
- **Source:** General research consensus
- **Basis:** Minimal impact on crash risk
- **Calculation:** Negligible increase

## Notes on Citation Format

- **DOI (Digital Object Identifier):** Permanent identifier for academic papers
- **URL:** Direct link to paper (may require institutional access)
- **Full citations:** Follow standard academic format (Author, Year, Title, Journal, Volume, Pages)

1. **Open Access:** Some papers (ETRR, Scientific Reports) are open access
2. **Institutional Access:** University libraries typically provide access
3. **ResearchGate/Academia.edu:** Authors may share preprints
4. **DOI Resolver:** Use DOI to find paper: https://doi.org/[DOI_NUMBER]
